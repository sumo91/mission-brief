# Source fidelity

Use this branch when a named source mixes authority states, contains material completed investigation, or cannot remain available after handoff.

## Separate source status

Read the source as mixed material rather than one authority block. Distinguish adopted decisions and applicable Authority Sources from confirmed facts, candidate approaches, and superseded material. Retaining a source preserves access, not authority.

Confirmed facts, dependencies, and causal findings remain Reference context unless an adopted decision or applicable Authority Source gives them a binding effect. Express that adopted effect in the Brief without relabeling the underlying investigation as authority.

## Make the handoff durable

Use a repository-relative pointer when the source will remain available. Name the categories it preserves and their status when a fresh Agent could otherwise mistake optional material for requirements.

When a temporary source is the only home of material information, preserve the minimum necessary facts in the Brief's `Context` or another appropriate durable source. Create a companion context record only when the material is substantial and the user-authorized repository write or an established convention provides a natural home.

For a confirmed causal finding, retain the actor or trigger, affected object, mechanism or relationship, and material consequence. Preserve enough to avoid repeating costly investigation without prescribing the eventual fix.

Keep candidate and historical routes in the labeled source. The Brief carries only their current adopted effect, if any.

This branch is complete when every material item is recoverable from a durable home with its authority status intact.
